# Continuity rules

The approved light novel controls events, personalities, relationships and world-building. A prototype, comment, old implementation report or generated dialogue is not a replacement for that source. When approved information is missing or conflicts, record the unresolved reference and seek author clarification; do not fill gaps with invented content.

Established protagonist mechanics supplied for production planning:

- Asakura Yuu returns to the **original summoning day** when a reset occurs.
- His physical state and **Character Level reset**.
- His memories and soul-bound **Skill Mastery persist**.
- Story information must never be revealed earlier than permitted by the approved light novel.

These rules are production continuity constraints, not permission to disclose them in an early episode. No cause, secret explanation or additional reset mechanics are established here. Do not infer them.

Track source chapter/revision, scene order, time/location, costume, props, injuries or physical condition only as established by the approved adaptation. Scene `continuityNotes` should record approved state on entry/exit and the reveal boundary without copying unpublished passages. Use versioned asset references so a later artwork update does not silently alter continuity.

Yuu's silver bracelet belongs to **Yuu**, not Shiori. Its meaning must not be invented or explained ahead of the source. Yuu and Shiori are childhood friends and are not intended as a romantic couple. Do not infer romantic framing from a prototype gesture. Keep canonical names and technical aliases aligned with [the character registry](CHARACTER_REGISTRY.md).

Before QC approval, check adjacent shot eyelines, screen direction, hands/held objects, character/prop placement, expression transitions, costume and audio speaker identity. Recheck the approved reveal order across the whole episode, including captions, narration, sound cues and background details.
