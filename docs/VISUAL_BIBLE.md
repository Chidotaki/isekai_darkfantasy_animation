# Visual bible

**Proposed production direction, pending design approval:** Japanese 2D anime, dark fantasy, limited animation and reusable puppet/cutout rigs. These are production goals, not approved canonical character designs.

Preserve the existing prototype silhouettes, palettes and proportions during foundation work. Prototype art is provisional and must not silently become the production master sheet. Future approved artwork should define consistent head/body proportions, facial landmarks, clothing layers, joint anchors, turn views and expression ranges before replacing a rig asset version.

Use clean silhouettes, controlled line weight and readable cel-shaded light/shadow groups. Build reusable layered environments with restrained atmospheric depth. Favor cinematic shot composition, stable screen direction and clear staging over camera motion on every line. Use held poses, anticipation, blinks, small expression transitions and selective secondary motion; dark fantasy does not require every interaction to look threatening.

Character performance follows the approved source. Yuu can smile, joke and react naturally; Shiori's childhood connection must not be turned into an assumed romance; Kairyū should remain approachable rather than an invented arrogant rival. Consult [continuity](CONTINUITY.md) before changing portrayal or revealing information.

Phase 2 will establish approved anime master sheets, expression sheets, palettes and a hybrid rendering strategy. No new artwork, prompts, master sheets or visual redesigns are part of Phase 1. Register approved replacements as new asset versions and review representative close-ups, action poses and full scenes before adopting them across episodes.
