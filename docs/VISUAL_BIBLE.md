# Visual bible

**Production direction:** Japanese 2D anime, dark fantasy, limited animation and reusable puppet/cutout rigs. The supplied Chapter 1 starter sheets for Asakura Yuu, Tachibana Kairyū and Minase Shiori are approved for current implementation. Other designs and expanded masters still require approval.

Preserve the approved starter silhouettes, palettes, faces and outfits: Yuu's black hair and dark hooded clothing, Kairyū's brown hair and silver armor with blue cape, Shiori's long dark hair and embroidered blue/silver robe. The active layered appearances use original sheet pixels. Keep the four historical prototypes compatible; their old artwork remains provisional and does not define production designs. Future masters should expand the approved views, separations and expression range without silently redesigning these characters.

Use clean silhouettes, controlled line weight and readable cel-shaded light/shadow groups. Build reusable layered environments with restrained atmospheric depth. Favor cinematic shot composition, stable screen direction and clear staging over camera motion on every line. Use held poses, anticipation, blinks, small expression transitions and selective secondary motion; dark fantasy does not require every interaction to look threatening.

Character performance follows the approved source. Yuu can smile, joke and react naturally; Shiori's childhood connection must not be turned into an assumed romance; Kairyū should remain approachable rather than an invented arrogant rival. Consult [continuity](CONTINUITY.md) before changing portrayal or revealing information.

Phase 2 provides a hybrid renderer and a noncanonical visual test now using the three approved starter references. Their source sheets, runtime appearances and derived rig bindings are registered separately. Front-view body parts and four painted portraits per character support restrained performance. These references are not complete production masters for all actions, angles or later arcs. Follow the [artwork pipeline](ARTWORK_PIPELINE.md), [rig standard](PUPPET_RIG_STANDARD.md) and [integration report](CHAPTER_1_STARTER_INTEGRATION.md). Register expanded approved artwork as new versions and inspect close-ups, action poses and full scenes before broader use.
